Chinese PMO calendar facts used by ICU
====================================

The Era/monthCode calendar table names the PMO publication for Chinese
calendar values in Gregorian 1900..2100 inclusive:
https://tc39.es/proposal-intl-era-monthcode/#table-calendar-types
https://pmo.cas.cn/xwdt2019/kpdt2019/202203/t20220309_6386774.html

The exact PMO page hosts and actively uses the calendar.js factual
lunarInfo sequence at https://pmo.cas.cn/images/calendar.js. The cached
header credits Jea Yang (JJonline@JJonline.Cn), calendar.js v1.0.3,
2014..2017. This ICU integration copies numeric calendar facts, with that
attribution, and contains independently written search/integration code.
It neither copies nor evaluates the retrieved JavaScript implementation.
The cached script declares no license grant; this integration makes no
claim about a license for that JavaScript implementation, PMO authorship
of that implementation, separate GB/T certification, or public-domain
status for the complete script. ICU/Unicode source notices are retained.

The PMO historical PDF supplies the initial January 1900 facts (lunar
1899 M12), which are outside the hosted sequence's first lunar year:
https://pmo.cas.cn/xwdt2019/kpdt2019/202203/P020250414456381274062.pdf
The first eleven lunar 1899 months are a private approximation outside
the mandatory Gregorian interval. They complete the enclosing arithmetic
year for January 1900. They are not acquired PMO 1899 observations.
The final zero-length 2101 M01 row is the boundary derived from the
published 2100 M12 length. It closes 2100 year limits and month addition;
it is not an acquired 2101 lunar year or an ordinary zero-day month.

The table owns calendar civil days, not new-moon/solar-term instants.
Its full data interval is [-25892,47875), in days since 1970-01-01, with
the primary required Gregorian interval [-25567,47847). Astronomical
calculation remains for dates/years outside the available data, except
the closing 2101 M01 start needed to close the final published year.
Dangi keeps its separate settings and astronomical calculation.

The pinned independent HKO comparison disagrees with the PMO-hosted
sequence on 30 dates in 2057 and lacks 2069-12-30. Those facts are retained
in packet provenance; HKO is corroboration, not substituted authority.
