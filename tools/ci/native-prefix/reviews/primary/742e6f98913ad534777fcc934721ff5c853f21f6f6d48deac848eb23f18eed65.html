PluralRuleSelect ( _locale_: a language tag, _type_: *"cardinal"* or *"ordinal"*, _notation_: a String, _compactDisplay_: *"short"* or *"long"*, _decimalString_: a decimal String, ): *"zero"*, *"one"*, *"two"*, *"few"*, *"many"*, or *"other"*

description
The returned String characterizes the plural category of _decimalString_ according to _type_, _notation_, and _compactDisplay_ for the corresponding _locale_.
