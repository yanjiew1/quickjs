calendar-integer.patch contains exactly the following accepted local changes:

Normalize Hebrew lunar parts and weekdays before the epoch

Hebrew startOfYear floors the month count but truncates negative lunar
parts and leaves a negative weekday remainder. This changes the year
zero postponements and the lengths of Cheshvan and Kislev.
Floor the whole days and normalize both remainders so the existing
postponement rules apply to years before the epoch as well.

Floor Indian calendar half-integer Julian dates

Indian handleComputeMonthStart converts a half-integer Julian date to
an integer by truncation. Negative dates move forward one day, breaking
calendar field round trips near Temporal's lower supported date.
Floor the Julian date before conversion so the returned integer is the
day before the month begins for dates on either side of the Julian epoch.
